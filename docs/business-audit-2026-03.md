## 9. ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS: Why Conversion Is Low

1. **No visible proof of work** — Case studies are missing. People buy based on results, not credentials.
2. **No social proof** — Testimonials missing. People trust others' experiences more than claims.
3. **Unclear value proposition** — "Full-Stack Developer & AI Architect" is generic. Why should I hire you vs. others?
4. **Services not defined** — Visitors don't know if you do their specific work (SaaS? Marketing sites? e-commerce?)
5. **Pricing hidden** — Budget questions stop prospects before they inquire.
6. **No trust-building content** — Blog drafts aren't published. Low authority signal.
7. **Weak SEO** — Not discoverable via search. Relying entirely on referrals/network.

**In short**: The site proves Leroy is *technically competent* but doesn't prove he can *solve client problems effectively*.
