## 🎬 Production Setup

### Equipment Needed
**Minimum:**
- Screen recording: OBS Studio (free) or ScreenFlow
- Mic: USB mic or AirPods Pro
- Editing: iMovie or DaVinci Resolve (free)

**Upgrade Path:**
- Camera: Webcam or phone camera
- Lighting: Ring light or natural light
- Editing: Final Cut Pro or Premiere Pro

### Recording Workflow
1. **Plan** (5 min) - Outline talking points
2. **Record** (10-20 min) - Screen + voiceover
3. **Edit** (15-30 min) - Cut dead air, add text overlays
4. **Thumbnail** (5 min) - Canva template
5. **Upload** (5 min) - YouTube + cross-post
6. **Embed** (2 min) - Add to portfolio blog

**Total time per video:** 45-60 minutes
